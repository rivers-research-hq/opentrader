## 2. Executive summary

**The FX arm is alive and accruing venue evidence.** Eight tracked FX caches were rewritten during the assessment session; the lanes are running on cron against the OANDA practice account; the warden, lifecycle registry, claims auction and scoreboard are genuinely wired to each other. The `--once`=REAL contract is **not violated by any cron site** — that part of the design holds.

**But every surface that is supposed to constrain or measure the FX arm is failing its own written rule, on four independent axes at once:**

1. **The 2026-08-29 "hardening" sweep caused two separate systemic regressions.** It left the SSRF/`guarded_urlopen` shadow **silently defeated in 17 of 31 files** that install it (`#230` D1) — and it **broke 7 modules outright** by inserting the guard import above `from __future__` (`#235` R1), which silently disabled the weekday accumulator cron **and map #92's already-wired VIX gate on the live fill path**. One of the project's live decision inputs is off by accident, and the guard that was supposed to protect egress mostly isn't running.
2. **"Venue is authoritative" breaks down at the surface.** Four researchers independently found the scoreboard/TUI/ledger misattributing, dropping, or mis-booking venue truth — a phantom **+3876.91** P&L from zero-price rows booked at cost 0 (`#234` D4), 10 double-booked fills and 119 orphan fills (`#229` D4/D5), the fill-size matcher living in **5 places, not 2 — including `build_accrual_store.py`, which writes misattributed tags into the experts' own evidence base** (`#229` D3), and a headline `realized_all = 0.0` that no one has traced to the journal (`#231` data flag).
3. **The alpha search's evidence is not currently admissible.** The fxexpert loop's *only* PASS (g151) is not cleanly evidenced — its gate file was rewritten 27 minutes after the loop logged FAIL, with no promotion row in `history.jsonl` (`#232` G2) — and `train.py:170-171` leaks test-fold statistics into normalization in all 163 generations (`#232` D1). Best-of-127 sits at z≈2.06, consistent with selection noise (`#232` G4).
4. **Doc drift and governance rot.** `ARCHITECTURE.md` is 36 days stale and wrong on facts it calls "single source of truth" (harness 4260 vs 4763 lines; `:5802` documented DOWN but listening); `CONTEXT.md` is 57% one bullet; three AGENTS.md claims are stale; the ToC claims ledger records zero per-phase spend against a 51,218 lifetime and has no checkpoint in 10 days (`#236`).

**Honest bottom line, consistent with the project's own record:** there is **no validated edge** (equity: four sources agree; FX: the one promotion is noise-consistent). On the **practice** account there is **no money at risk today** — but none of the top defects is caused by the account being practice. Each converts directly into money loss the moment the book is funded.

## 4. Prioritized action backlog

Ordered by the human's rubric: **risk to live account → quick wins → structural debt.**

### Tier 1 — Risk to the live account (state-independent; transfers to a funded book)

**1. The 2026-08-29 hardening sweep broke 7 modules and silently disabled a live harness gate.**
*Severity: High.* The `from security.guards import …` line was inserted above `from __future__ import annotations` in `data/acquire.py:11`, `data/falsify.py:11`, `data/gpu_falsify.py:13`, `data/vix_gate.py:29`, `mot/hive.py:23`, `scenarios/neural.py:23`, `scenarios/train_generator.py:14` — a `SyntaxError` that `ast.parse` misses (only `compile()` catches it). Consequence: the weekday 14:30 accumulator cron fails at step 1, and **map #92's VIX gate is wired (`harness.py:2553-2573`, `:2630-2633`) but permanently `vix_allow=True`** because its import sits inside `except Exception` (`harness.py:2562,2572-2573`). *Fix:* move the guard import below the future import (mechanical), then decide the VIX gate's mode (its `strict` default was already falsified in-file, 0 fills). *Owner:* #206 / #92 / #83. *Evidence:* `#235` R1.

**2. The SSRF/`guarded_urlopen` shadow is silently defeated in 17 of 31 files.**
*Severity: High (security).* `exchange/oanda.py:14` installs the guard, then `:25` `from urllib.request import Request, urlopen` rebinds the name and `:124` uses the unguarded stock function — so `validate_url` never runs and the Bearer token goes to whatever host `config/oanda_keys.json` names. Same class in 16 other files; `harness.py` is intact. *Fix:* import order (install the shadow after stdlib imports) + a test asserting `exchange.oanda.urlopen is security.guards.guarded_urlopen`. *Evidence:* `#230` D1.

**3. The watchdog can never flatten a short — active now.**
*Severity: High.* `fx_watchdog.py:63-66` compares the **signed** venue net to `abs(units)`; any short yields `abs(net−units)=2U` → "deferred" forever. Shorts are ~45% of fxexp flow and those lanes carry **no SL/TP by design** (`fx_expert_lane.py:20-21`), so short exposure has no protection of any kind. Live log: `USD_JPY venue net -1241.0 != book 1241.0 — deferred`. *Fix:* use signed-vs-signed (or `abs` on both), plus decide whether the no-SL/TP fxexp design survives this finding. *Evidence:* `#229` D1.

**4. `_ensure_protection` logs "protection restored" when the re-attach failed.**
*Severity: High.* `fx_runner.py:147-152` checks `"stopLossOrderRejectTransaction" not in r`, but `_request` returns `{}` on network failure and `{"_error": …}` on HTTP error (`exchange/oanda.py:128-139`) — so a dead network and a venue rejection both satisfy the "ok" disjunct. A naked position is believed protected and nothing re-checks. *Fix:* treat `{}`/`_error` as failure, not success. *Evidence:* `#229` D2.

**5. A live 5-minute REAL-mode trail/TP closer contradicts the project's own measured verdict.**
*Severity: High (governance).* `fx-trail-check.timer` runs `fx_trail_check.py --once` (= REAL) every 5 min, and the lanes call it live — while the project's own A/B concludes "every risk-machinery variant loses to hold-to-rebalance" (`docs/agents/research/fx-warden-2026-09-08.md:105-127`). The module's justification (selective ≠ uniform stops) is a variant that A/B did **not** measure. *Fix:* measure the selective variant before it touches a live book, or switch the timer to dry. **Human-gated.** *Evidence:* `#231` D4.

**6. `get_current_price` still fails open to an untimestamped, unbounded-age mark.**
*Severity: Medium-High.* `exchange/oanda.py:256` returns `_price_cache.get(symbol)` on any empty fetch; the non-fetch writer is a *bar close* (`:212`), up to a day old for the `1d` bars the daily runner asks for. This is the documented `STOP_LOSS_ON_FILL_LOSS` mechanism, narrowed but not closed, and silent. Deleting the fallback alone is unsafe (`fx_runner.py:419-425` would `TypeError` on `None`). *Fix:* None-guard at the call site **and** drop or timestamp-bound the fallback. *Evidence:* `#230` D2.

**7. Tagless venue fills are misattributed by fill size — in 5 places, including the evidence base.**
*Severity: High.* 96 of 129 tagless `venue-reconciliation` rows postdate the 09-03 tradeID upgrade; the size matcher (`q≥5000→crash, ≥2000→h1-mom, else→mom-k5`) lives in `tui/index.js:108-111,456`, `dashboard.py:612-617`, `tui.py:79-89`, `build_accrual_store.py:143-145`, and `fx_crashtest.py:78-85`. Every 2000u lane collapses into `h1-mom`; `c08-fade` can never receive a tagless close; and **`build_accrual_store.py` persists the misattributed tag into the experts' training/gating evidence.** *Fix (immediate):* fix the tag-resolution chain and add a loud "unattributed" bucket instead of the silent `mom-k5` default. *Fix (structural):* one shared resolver; see Tier 3. *Evidence:* `#229` D3, `#234` D1-D4.

**8. Latent `NameError` mid-rebalance leaves real orders with no ledger/state record.**
*Severity: High.* `fx_expert_lane.py:373` uses `net` before its only binding at `:384` — the lane crashes after orders are sent, before `_append_ledger`/state write. This matches the 119 fills that exist in the ledger only as tagless venue rows. *Fix:* initialize `net` before the flip branch; add a lock + a regression test. *Evidence:* `#229` D4, D7.

**9. `price = 0.0` on 128 per-tradeID close rows defeats reconcile dedup → double-booked fills.**
*Severity: Medium-High.* `fx_expert_lane.py:306,368` record closes with no price; the reconcile dedup keys on price within 1e-9 (`fx_runner.py:274-277`), so the same fill is appended twice (10 verified). The TUI then skips the original (`tui/index.js:121`) and counts only the misattributed copy. *Fix:* write the venue close price; fix the dedup key. *Evidence:* `#229` D5.

**10. `instability()` raises `UnboundLocalError` on the venue-wide maintenance branch, aborting the Warden run.**
*Severity: High (silent).* `fx_warden.py:698` writes `out["maintenance_window"]` but `out` is first bound at `:726`; the branch meant to *suppress* false halts instead crashes the run (callers swallow the exception). *Fix:* bind `out = {}` at function top. *Evidence:* `#231` D1.

**11. Every documented schedule is 5 hours off.**
*Severity: Medium.* Box is US/Central with no `CRON_TZ`; log mtimes prove local firing. "mom-k5 17:10 UTC" actually fires **22:10 UTC** — after the Friday FX close. *Fix:* either set `CRON_TZ=UTC` or rewrite the docs to local time; re-audit the calendar/blackout reasoning. *Evidence:* `#229` D9.

**12. The warden's event-proximity risk term is silently dead.**
*Severity: Medium-High.* `fetch_ff_upcoming.py:59-66` emits `date_label`/`country`/lowercase `impact`; `fx_warden.py:718-723` reads `date`/`currency`/`impact in ("High","Med")` — never true, so `event_48h` is always false and the `+10.0` stress term never fires (confirmed in `records.jsonl`). *Fix:* align producer/consumer keys. *Evidence:* `#233` D2.

**13. `fetch_fred_cond.py` would delete 9 FRED series it never restores.**
*Severity: High (data-loss foot-gun).* `fetch_fred_cond.py:95` runs `DELETE FROM exog WHERE series LIKE 'FRED:%'` then re-inserts only its own 8-name list — the store holds 17 `FRED:*`, including `FRED:BAMLEMHYHYLCRPIUSOAS` which the warden reads for EM-HY stress (vanishes silently behind `if not rows: continue`). *Fix:* reconcile the SERIES list before the next run. *Evidence:* `#233` D3.

**14. `fx_trail_check` closes *other* lanes' trades and stamps them with the caller's tag.**
*Severity: Medium (latent).* `fx_trail_check.py:62` filters by `startswith("fxexp-")` while ignoring `my_tag`, yet stamps the close with the caller's tag (`:116`). Latent (zero `trail-close` rows yet) but fires on the first trail trigger. *Fix:* filter by `my_tag`. *Evidence:* `#231` D3, `#229` D6.

### Tier 3 — Structural debt (do after the above)

**22. Repair the fxexpert evaluation before trusting any promotion.**
`train.py:170-171` uses rows `0…n_train` of the full pair-major panel for `mu`/`sd` instead of the fold's train rows (`X[idx_keep[tr_idx]]`) — future data enters normalization in all 163 generations. The lone PASS (g151) has out-of-band provenance; 163 evaluations reuse 3 fixed folds with no multiple-testing correction; and the cost model omits financing/swap, exactly the term a 57-leg multi-day FX book pays. *Order:* fix D1 + re-baseline → then construction/sizing → then new inputs. *Evidence:* `#232` D1/D6, G1-G4.

**23. One shared lane-attribution resolver (replaces the 5 size matchers).**
The matcher's blast radius (Tier-1 item 7) is a design symptom. Build a single resolver that reads the venue txn's `clientExtensions`/`orderID` chain, with a loud "unattributed" bucket, and route all five consumers (TUI, dashboard, `tui.py`, `build_accrual_store.py`, `fx_crashtest.py`) through it. This is ToC Q05. *Evidence:* `#229` D3, `#230` Q5.

**24. Split the `harness.py` monolith (4763 lines, 51 defs, 2 units sharing one file).**
At minimum extract the regime/attribution block (`:2774`/`:2789`/`:2847`) out of the middle of the file. The doc's "deferred split" has been deferred across another 503 lines of growth. *Evidence:* `#236` D15.

**25. A minimal test runner + CI.**
No `pyproject.toml`/`pytest.ini`/`conftest.py`/`.github/` exists; "run the tests" is ambiguous; the fatal-defect class (silent hold, order rejection, >15 bps slippage) has no change gate. Add a `pytest.ini` + a pre-push `pytest -q` hook. *Evidence:* `#236` D14.

**26. Resolve the data store's single-writer contradiction.**
`store.duckdb` has **4 writers** (full rebuild, FRED, fx_expert_lane bars, fx_expand_universe) against its own "never a second writer" docstring; the Parquet "durable mirror" is 5 days / 5.1M rows behind the DuckDB readers actually open; `--skip-venue` wipes venue tables; the ledger path is frozen at 12.6%. Decide the owner, then make one rebuild script the only writer (or document multi-writer deliberately). *Evidence:* `#233` D4/D5/D7.

**27. Make the ToC claims ledger actually gate.**
Per-phase spend is inert (all 8 phases `spent: 0` vs lifetime 51,218); two phases open at once; no checkpoint in 10 days while 14 questions accrued; the findings-log layer is empty while findings are written into `openQuestions`. Enforce: close prior phase before opening a new one; checkpoint gate before a `[known]` promotion; route findings to `04-raw`. *Evidence:* `#236` D18-D22.

**28. Break the `tui.py` load-bearing dependency.**
`tui.py` is simultaneously a documented wrong-client trap **and** a live import dependency (`dashboard.py:636 from tui import ledger_performance` → `/api/fx-lanes` polled every 5s), and its FIFO produces the +3876.91 phantom. Move `ledger_performance`/`attribute_lane` into a proper module, then let the human decide `tui.py`'s fate. *Evidence:* `#234` D8, D4.

**29. Scoreboard metric integrity: resolve `realized_all = 0.0`.**
The trained lanes show an exactly-flat venue realized (implausible for 47 round trips at 17% WR) while the ledger alternatives are provably wrong (+3876.91 / −4.00). One read-only venue-journal walk per tag settles whether it's genuine or an attribution break — before the Friday 2026-09-11 cut reads a backtest-shaped scoreboard. Also fix give-back (unbounded >1 / negative) and the MFE gap for planned lanes. *Evidence:* `#231` data flag + D8/D9, `#234` OQ1.

## 5. What this means for next steps

Three conclusions stand out from the whole sweep:

- **The highest-leverage single action is the two hardening-sweep repairs** (Tier-1 items 1–2): one mechanical import-order fix across ~24 files that re-enables a live gate *and* restores the egress guard. It is the rare defect that is cheap, safe, and unblocks two otherwise-separate failures at once.
- **Do not trust the fxexpert gate's verdicts until item 22 lands.** The live g151 lane is rebalanced every 5 trading days from a checkpoint whose promotion never reached the loop's own history — the Friday cut is the only guardrail, and the scoreboard it will read is itself suspect (item 29). This is the "decision risk is high, account risk is low" quadrant: bounded today on practice money, but the *steering* is currently on noise.
- **The honest position is unchanged and should stay that way.** Nothing in this sweep found an edge; it found that the machinery for *detecting* an edge is the part that needs the work. That is consistent with the project's own record (no validated wide-universe edge; buy-and-hold is the benchmark) and argues for repairing measurement before expanding the search.
