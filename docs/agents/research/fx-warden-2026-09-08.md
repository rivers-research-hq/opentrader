## The mechanisms

**1. Expectation-conditioned reward (QB/RB rule).** Daily `plan` reads the
news digest + instability table + lane states and sets per-lane
`expected_pnl_pct` (direction, conviction, rationale). `score` computes in
code: `miss = actual − expected`; a negative miss adds `−|expected|`
harshness. Period actual = realized delta since the plan-time snapshot +
current uPL (accounting fixed 2026-09-09 — was all-time, which made scores
carry pre-period history). Plan snapshots `period_start_nav` + per-tag
realized. Lanes the model omits are backfilled flat (lane-coverage
validator).

**2. Shadow cut list.** Expectation-adjusted bad period → watch; second →
probation; third → escalate (human's Friday list). Recovery → reprieved.

**3. Instability watch v0.2.** Per-currency composite (code-computed): OANDA
halt 30pts (halt = status≠tradeable AND price >30min stale AND persists 2
consecutive runs), vol-spike ratio 25, 5d shock 25, event<48h 10, global
bond/vix stress 10 (from the extended panel: VIX, US HY OAS, **EM HY OAS**,
**DGS10**, **T10Y2Y**). Wired into plan/observe prompts; plans store the
non-calm snapshot.

**4. Sizing-fidelity audit.** Daily with score: per-leg actual USD notional
vs intended |weight|×NOTIONAL from venue truth; dust-excluded; $0 non-dust
legs flagged missing. First runs: 90.7–96.2%.

**5. Anti-hallucination.** Notes carry a `verified` bit — every % cited must
match a venue value (first finding: the 4B invented derived % ~100% of the
time; the verifier gates the mid-train corpus).

**6. Model lineage.** Every record/note is stamped with its producing model
(`warden-4.2-8b`, or `warden-qwen-fallback` during failovers) — swap-safe
receipts for the mid-train.

## Conviction auction — non-overlapping lanes (2026-09-09, live)

Human directive: lanes must not overlap; conviction decides ownership.
`strategies/lane_claims.py`: each lane writes per-pair conviction (|weight|)
at its run; assignment is deterministic argmax per pair (promoted order
breaks ties), frozen per period, recomputed on change. Registry:
`data/fx_expert/claims.json`.

- **Verified live: 53 symbols held, 0 overlapping pairs** (18/22/18 per
  lane) — the netting war is structurally dead.
- **Execution core rewritten** in `fx_expert_lane.py`: reductions/exits are
  per-tradeID closes (surgical — netting orders FIFO-close other lanes'
  older fragments; proven when 229 fragments closed surgically); market
  orders only for same-sign adds/opens with the foreign-net guard. Legacy
  positions on pairs lost to another lane are exited via tradeID closes.
  The consolidation path is superseded (flag retained as a no-op).
- **Disclosed trade-offs**: per-lane dollar-neutrality is broken by the
  partition — the account carries a directional residual (the assignment is
  unconstrained; a balance-constrained auction is the v0.3 refinement).
  Sibling models agree on ~90% of rankings, so the auction differentiates
  on the disagreement set — books still correlate until the mid-train
  diverges them.
- **Pushback recorded on cut timing**: partitioned books carry ~19 legs
  each — a one-week cut decides on noise. Recommendation: two-week windows
  or IC-based scoring before cutting. Human's call.

## TP/SL A/B — the data says hold (2026-09-09)

Human question: why no TP/SL, and should long-horizon books carry a pseudo
trailing stop? Now measured: the OOS score export (`oos_scores_*.npz` in the
walkforward) feeds `fxexpert/trailing_ab.py` — the rank book simulated per
5-day period with per-leg daily mark-to-market, trail/TP triggers in ATR
units, identical costs across variants, ~28k leg-periods over 3 OOS folds:

| variant | PF | Sharpe |
|---|---|---|
| baseline (hold to rebalance) | **0.604** | **−1.83** |
| trail 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 / 3.0 ATR | 0.560–0.594 | −1.90 to −2.09 |
| TP 2.0 / 3.0 ATR | 0.599 / 0.603 | −1.86 / −1.84 |
| trail 1.5 + TP 3.0 | 0.580 | −1.98 |

**Every risk-machinery variant loses to hold-to-rebalance.** Structural
reason: the book is rank-weighted both sides — stops realize temporary
adverse marks on legs the 10-day horizon recovers, and TPs cap the legs that
pay for the shorts. This independently re-confirms the project's oldest
standing finding (R1 tournament era: "forced exits destroy returns").

Verdict: the live book keeps hold-to-rebalance; the lane's no-SL/TP design
is now *measured*, not assumed. The A/B harness (`trailing_ab.py`) stays —
any future risk variant gets the same test before touching the lane.

## MFE tracker + give-back ratio (2026-09-09, live)

The scoreboard now tracks per-lane **peak favorable excursion** during the
period — the hourly observe persists the max uPL per tag in the warden
state, and the daily score computes the give-back ratio:
(peak − current) / peak. Three columns added to the scoreboard per lane:
current uPL, peak uPL, give-back %.

Why: the hold-to-rebalance design means positions swing — a leg can peak
at +3σ and round-trip to zero. The give-back ratio separates **selection
skill** (was the pick good — high peak) from **exit-policy cost** (how much
was returned — high give-back). The cut reads both axes: a lane with high
MFE but high give-back has good picks and bad exits (mid-train's job);
a lane with low MFE entirely has bad picks (the cut's job).

Verified live (two consecutive hourly runs): the peaks persist correctly
across runs, give-back ratios compute, and the first transition (g138
−$12.73 → −$12.68, 0.0% give-back) shows the books are near their peaks.

## Simulated trail/TP via tradeID closes (2026-09-09, live)

Human directive: simulate TP/SL triggers in code and close by tradeID —
per-leg precision without server-side orders. Shipped as
`strategies/fx_trail_check.py` + `fx-trail-check.timer` (every 5 min).

Architecture:
- Each fxexp trade's peak/trough is tracked in `trail_state_<tag>.json`
  (entry price from the OANDA trade record, ATR from the accrual store)
- Trail: price retraces 2.0 ATR from peak (long) or trough (short)
- TP: price hits entry ± 3.0 ATR
- Triggered legs close via per-tradeID surgical close (zero netting
  ambiguity) — the same mechanism proven in the 229-fragment reset
- Freed margin redeployed at the next rebalance; captured PnL attributed
  per lane tag
- The A/B verdict stands: uniform mechanical stops hurt the portfolio PF.
  This is the *per-leg selective* version — different mechanism, different
  result possible. The A/B harness (`trailing_ab.py`) stays for the
  backtest; the live trail check runs at 5-min resolution (catches moves
  between the hourly warden runs).
